# PJR Relic / Resonance dialogue pass

Baseline: Run188 / commit ab62a8b7c44938bc24fb87ef7e64f5442fd7e58d.

This pass is deliberately save-compatible. It changes dialogue/sign text only and does not renumber or repurpose species, items, flags, vars, maps, encounters, or progression gates.

## Story thread reinforced

- Elm's existing ancient Resonance opening now carries through Violet and Sprout Tower.
- Ruins of Alph researchers/NPCs establish Resonance, Relics, Unown and the early First Voice tease.
- Kurt/Slowpoke Well aftermath introduces the stolen ancient-material thread.
- Lake of Rage explicitly identifies Team Rocket's signal as forced Resonance.
- Mahogany scientists/Petrel reveal stolen Alph research and the Radio Tower amplifier plan.
- Archer's Radio Tower climax targets the buried Alph network; the ruins answer after his defeat.
- Ecruteak/Burned Tower/Tin Tower connect Ho-Oh, the beasts, old tower foundations and natural Resonance.
- Dragon's Den contrasts bond-driven Resonance with Team Rocket's forced method.
- Lance/Oak carry the thread through the first League clear.
- Pewter Museum and Lavender Radio let Kanto react to the awakened Johto network.

The existing post-League RELICS battles are not relocated in this pass. The planned world-spanning sequence (Burned Tower / Mt. Mortar / Whirl Islands / hidden Alph / Ilex Forest / sacred spring / Alphoracle / Relic Ho-Oh) remains a separate structural change for the fresh-save version.
