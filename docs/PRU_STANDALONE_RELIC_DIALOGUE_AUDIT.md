**Canonical game baseline:** PJR \`pjr-rebuild\` via the PRU development branch (source audited at \`22b95ad53f087770e21a03d1c0496c117fed5300\`).

## Locked narrative continuity
1. This is **one new adventure**, beginning in New Bark Town with **Pikachu** given by Elm. The player has not cleared PKR, PHR, PJR, PRL, Hoenn, Kanto, the Sevii Islands or any previous championship. No prior party, prior wealth, badge carryover, inter-ROM transfer, Aunt Rosie, Birch starter reset, regional storage/banking, S.S. Legacy or old protagonist.
2. **Johto first, Kanto second**. Preserve PJR's Johto maps, Team Rocket radio-tower plot, Kimono Girls, legendary story, first League victory and entry to Kanto. PJR's postgame Sinjoh and other established maps stay; text may need adaptation, even where maps and battle triggers remain unchanged.
3. One continuous phenomenon: **Resonance** is the measurable ancient signal; **Relics** are species, traits and artifacts influenced by it. Johto's Ruins of Alph is the original research hub. Kanto fossils/Silph/Cinnabar uncover additional manifestations **within PRU's current story**, not through a previous Kanto-only ROM.
4. No assumption that the player **previously** caught, raised, donated, boxed or transferred any Pokémon. NPCs may mention history in distant places, including Hoenn; those are *world lore*, not player history.
5. Keep Elm's **original Togepi Egg**, Mr. Pokémon's Mystery Egg investigation, hatching, Kimono dialogue and related progress flags. **NPC gifts, eggs and prizes are welcome alongside separate wild/evolution/encounter acquisition paths** (user clarification, 16 Oct 2026). Initial Pikachu remains the sole selectable first starter; gift species must never be gift-exclusive.
6. Normal species and custom Relics are catchable/evolvable within Johto and Kanto as their maps unlock. Hoenn-exclusive geography, badges, Hoenn regional league and Hoenn champion-family plot are not playable in PRU.
7. Preserve established Johto PJR relic chain after first League: **R → E → L → I → C → S** (Raikou, Entei, Lugia, Unown I, Celebi, Suicune), then **Alphoracle**, **Ancient Rainbow Crest** and **Ho-Oh**. Additional catchable legendaries unlocked by Oak must complement—not replace, duplicate or preempt—those existing events.
8. The player's first E4 run is PJR's Johto championship. Further leagues and the Relic Battle Hall are PRU content built on that progression, with replay dialogue and postgame flags checked separately.
9. PRU includes advanced PJR plot Pokémon (e.g. Arceus, Regieleki, Regidrago). Do **not** delete them merely because the core National Dex planning starts at #001–#386. Resolve expanded Pokédex indexing/availability separately.
10. Any reuse of PRL Kanto lore is **adaptation**, not importing an entire parallel Kanto save-game plot or overwriting Johto progression.

## Narrative chapters for PRU — one continuous game
1. **Johto opening / the first signal:** Oak's instruments detect Resonance; Elm gives a promising Pikachu; local scientists do not yet understand why some species show unusual evolution. The player is a novice. Silver's theft and the Mystery Egg remain PJR's existing story beats.
2. **Field discoveries:** Ruins of Alph stones, Ecruteak tower lore, Dragon's Den, traditional starter families in the wild and early Relic babies provide escalating evidence. Keep the surprise of individual Relic evolutions; no dialogue promising the player already owns them.
3. **Radio Tower crisis:** Rocket interference boosts ancient signals. After defeat, the network continues naturally, leading to a new research era. No implication that the same Rocket organisation simply takes over Silph later.
4. **First League / the signal awakens:** Lance and Oak witness Alph's response to the Champion battle, introducing the R→E→L→I→C→S search and Alphoracle. This is PRU's **first** Hall of Fame.
5. **Kanto archaeology / extended investigation:** Oak correlates Johto evidence with museum fossils, Mt. Moon, Silph archives, Bill and Cinnabar. He makes other remaining legendaries discoverable across **both playable regions**. If Kanto Rocket documents remain, they are *past evidence or a surviving cell*, not an unexplained reset of Giovanni's organisation. No travel to Hoenn.
6. **Ho-Oh resolution and Sinjoh:** Alphoracle and Red's Ancient Rainbow Crest lead to Ho-Oh. Steven's references to Hoenn are historical scholarship; the player's storyline stays in Johto/Kanto. Preserve PJR's Sinjoh and Arceus events.
7. **Ultimate challenges:** The four championship tiers, 16 Ultimate Gym partners and Relic Battle Hall (J&J, S&N, G&M, S&W, B&B) reference achievements **inside PRU**. The dialogue never mentions previous ROMs, regional restarts or inter-ROM transfers.

## Non-negotiable engineering and QA gates

- Preserve every original PJR story flag and progression-critical interaction by default. Re-use existing narrative flags only after mapping exact semantics; new Kanto evidence needs unique flags, not flags shared with PJR rocket/league/legendary chains.
- Make *one* authoritative NPC per milestone: Elm/Alph research for Johto discovery, Oak for broader cross-region sightings, Steven for Sinjoh archaeology, existing Kimono Girls for guardian lore.
- Branch dialogue by **the player's actual PRU flags**, e.g. \`FLAG_IS_CHAMPION\`, \`FLAG_IS_KANTO_CHAMPION\`, \`FLAG_PJR_RELIC_HO_OH\`, not donor-ROM chapter counts or PRL saved variables.

- GBA text uses correctly escaped \`\\n\`, \`\\p\`, and \`\\l\` script control sequences; inspect displayed page width and line count. No orphan flags, missing script labels or dead-end mandatory dialogues.
- **No Hoenn playable map/warps/transfers**, Hoenn badges or family transfer text in PRU. Other characters may discuss Hoenn as a distant place, and Hoenn Pokémon may live in Johto/Kanto.
- Verify whether PJR's existing FRLG donor maps/scripts are enabled in \`make hns\`; don't assume that all copied FRLG files correspond to live in-game locations.
